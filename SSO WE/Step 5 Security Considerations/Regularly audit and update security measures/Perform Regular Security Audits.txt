
    Define Security Policies: Define security policies and procedures that outline the steps to be taken to ensure the security of the system.
    Identify Assets: Identify the assets that need to be protected, such as sensitive data, intellectual property, and critical infrastructure.
    Identify Threats: Identify the potential threats to the system, such as malware, phishing, and social engineering attacks.
    Assess Risks: Assess the risks associated with each threat and prioritize them based on their potential impact and likelihood.
    Implement Controls: Implement security controls to mitigate the identified risks, such as firewalls, intrusion detection systems, and access controls.
    Test Controls: Test the security controls to ensure they are working as intended and identify any weaknesses or vulnerabilities.
    Monitor and Review: Monitor and review the security controls regularly to ensure they are up-to-date and effective.
